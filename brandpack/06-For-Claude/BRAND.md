# Angels Oasis — brand brief for Claude

Use this file whenever you write or design anything for Angels Oasis. Values were read from angelsoasisclhf.com in October 2026. Items marked (new) are recommendations that go beyond the live site.

## The brand in one paragraph

Angels Oasis is a licensed Congregate Living Health Facility (CLHF) providing 24-hour skilled nursing and medically complex care across Southern California. It offers hospital-level care in a real home: six private rooms, a 3:1 caregiver-to-resident ratio, and a full clinical team on site around the clock.

- Tagline: **Home-Like Care. Hospital-Level Expertise.** Always two sentences, title case, each with a period. Never paraphrase or shorten it.
- Mission: "To provide exceptional care for the mentally alert and physically disabled residents and maintain a safe environment that feels just like home."
- Boilerplate: "Angels Oasis operates licensed Congregate Living Health Facilities across Southern California, each held to the same standard of 24-hour medical care in a warm, residential setting."

## Voice

Sound like the charge nurse a family hopes to meet: calm, kind, clearly good at the job.

- **Warm.** People before procedures. "So you never have to choose between quality care and comfort."
- **Steady.** Reassure with facts, not adjectives or exclamation marks.
- **Clinically exact.** Name services properly; state numbers plainly.
- **Plainspoken.** Short sentences; no jargon a worried family would have to look up.

Writing rules:

- Write **Angels Oasis** (two words, no apostrophe, never all caps). Legal lines: **Angels Oasis CLHF**. Spell out "Congregate Living Health Facility (CLHF)" on first mention.
- "Resident" or "your loved one" to families; "patient" only in referral contexts.
- "24-hour" before a noun; "3:1 caregiver-to-resident ratio"; phone as (323) 213-2831.
- Calls to action, exactly: Schedule a Visit, Take a Virtual Tour, Learn More About Our Services, Refer a Patient, Notify Me.
- Headlines (new): Title Case when short; sentence case for questions and long lines.
- Claims: no promised outcomes; no "best" or "#1" without a source; comparisons need a factual basis; never a resident's name, face, diagnosis or story without signed authorization; testimonials only if real and permitted.

## Graphic language

- **Wing marker:** the mark at about 30 px wide replaces bullets in feature lists and precedes every section eyebrow.
- **Pills:** fully rounded Sage, uppercase Inter SemiBold in Deep Forest. For payer types, tags, short facts.
- **Buttons:** pill-shaped. Primary = Forest with white text. Secondary = Sage with Deep Forest text. Text link = Forest, underlined, with an arrow.
- **Cards:** white or Cream, 1 px hairline, 10 px radius, 20 px padding. Testimonial cards are Deep Plum with gold stars and a Fraunces Light quote.
- **Referral card:** Light Sage top with "Refer a Patient" and the phone number in Fraunces Bold Plum; white bottom with contact details.
- **Sage band:** a full-width Sage strip that closes a hero or page with one line of information.
- **Service ribbon:** one line of service names in Fraunces separated by a ✱.
- **Icons:** line icons, 24 px grid, 1.75 px stroke, round caps, no fill (Lucide matches). Plum or Deep Mauve on light, Cream on Plum.
- **Radii:** 100 px pills, 10 px cards and photos, 6 px form fields. Shadows barely there or absent.
- **Never:** gradients, glows, emoji as icons, clip-art angels or crosses, colored side stripes on cards.

## Photography

Show care in progress and rooms that look like a home: warm natural light, people looking at each other, soft whites and creams. Avoid cold clinical blue, equipment without people, watermarked or obviously stock images, heavy filters, and any identifiable resident without signed authorization. When type sits on a photo, darken that area so Cream text holds 4.5:1. Photos from the current site are in `04-Website-Photos`; the San Jacinto exterior carries an MLS watermark and should be replaced.

## Layout recipes

- **Slide or page on Plum:** reversed logo top-left, Cream Fraunces headline, Sage labels, Sage or Forest pill for the action.
- **Slide or page on Cream/Linen:** wing eyebrow, Fraunces Light headline in Ink, Inter body, white hairline cards, Forest primary button.
- **Social post (1080 × 1350):** 72 px margins; logo bottom-left; action or phone bottom-right. Patterns: brand statement (photo over Plum), service spotlight (Cream), testimonial (Deep Plum).
- **Referral-facing pieces:** always include the referral card and accepted payers.
